Mission

Forest fire detection & response

Detect fires seconds after ignition, coordinate brigades, and keep crews safe.

Response

Coordinate an effective response in Overwatch

  • Brigade coordination

    Plan, task and report from one live map, the same map the crews see in the field.

  • Crew safety

    Monitor crew locations, escape routes, and fire conditions.

  • Aerial support

    Autonomous missions with the brigade’s own drones for persistent aerial intelligence.

  • Fire spread modeling

    Sentinel weather data informs spread forecasts and response plans.

  • Incident reports

    Timestamped detections, actions, and footage, from alert to stand-down.

Detection

Early fire detection across your perimeter

  • Ground sensing

    Gas and acoustic sensors detect combustion and smoldering seconds after ignition.

  • Long-range surveillance

    Sentinel detects smoke and keeps eyes on it.

  • Verified alerts

    Each detection is cross-checked across sensors to filter out false alarms before an alert goes out.

  • Cause of fire

    Review detections and footage to investigate the fire’s cause.

1 m²

Our system can detect a fire smaller than one square meter, over 140,000 times smaller in area than a single pixel in NASA’s VIIRS fire monitoring data.

One VIIRS pixel: 375 × 375 m at nadir, 140,625 m²
