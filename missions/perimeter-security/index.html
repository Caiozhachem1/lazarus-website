Mission

Perimeter security

Autonomous surveillance, active deterrence, and coordinated response.

What it covers

Complete coverage of your perimeter

  • Perimeter intrusion

    Learns your vehicles and routines. Flags suspicious and after-hours activity.

  • Line crossing

    Logs movement along the fence. Alerts on crossings.

  • Loitering

    Flags anyone lingering at a gate past your set limit.

  • Restricted zones

    Monitor designated areas for unauthorized entry.

  • Theft and tampering

    Flags removal or interference. Preserves the evidence.

  • Arson & sabotage

    Early warning of fire and threats to critical assets.

What you get

Fewer losses. Faster response

Active deterrence

Faster response

Efficient patrols
