What We Detect

A unified sensing and AI platform that continuously learns your perimeter to detect, verify, and understand emerging threats in real time.

Perimeter intrusion

Detect unauthorized access across remote perimeters. Lazarus learns what your vehicles and operations look like, and alerts when something doesn’t belong.

Theft & tampering

Monitor equipment, materials, and infrastructure for unauthorized removal or interference. Capture a clean evidence trail for investigations and claims.

Fire & arson

Gas sensors detect fires at the earliest stage, minutes before smoke or flame are visible to cameras. In cases of arson, the system captures a complete, timestamped evidence trail for criminal investigations.

Environmental monitoring

About Us

Our mission is to protect the physical world

Large parts of the world's critical infrastructure still rely on cameras that miss threats and patrols that can't keep up. Fire, sabotage, intrusion, theft - the risks are growing and the gaps are widening.

LEO satellite connectivity and modern AI made it possible to build something that didn't exist a few years ago: an autonomous monitoring system that deploys anywhere, connects from anywhere, and detects threats in seconds. No infrastructure required. No blind spots.

That's what we built. We design our own hardware, write our own software, and train our own AI - because the problems we solve don't have off-the-shelf answers. Our team has shipped mission-critical systems across self-driving vehicles, autonomous drones, semiconductor research, and defense tech.
